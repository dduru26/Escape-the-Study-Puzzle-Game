# Escape the Study

## What the game is

You wake up locked in a small study and have to get out. The room has a bunch of objects you can poke at, and a few of them actually do something. Solve the five puzzles hidden around the room and the door finally unlocks so you can leave. That's it — one room, one door, five things to figure out.


## The five puzzle tasks

You have to do all five. There's a progress counter on screen (`Puzzle Progress: X / 5`) so you can see how many are left.

1. **Turn on the lamp** — the room starts dark. There's a lamp that's faintly lit, and clicking it powers everything up. Nothing else really works until you do this first.
2. **Open the drawers in the right order** — there's a dresser with a few drawers. A picture on the wall hints at the order. Get it wrong and they slam shut with a buzzer and you start that one over.
3. **Rotate the object to line up the symbol** — one object (a framed picture / vase) has a symbol on it. Spin it until the symbol matches the one drawn on the wall.
4. **Put the object where it belongs** — pick up a small item and drop it on the surface that lights up / has the matching colour.
5. **Pull the lever** — this one only turns on (goes green) once the other four are done. Pull it and the door opens.

No puzzle tells you what to do in words — it's all colours, symbols, lights and sound. The only text on screen is the progress counter.

## Controls

| Key / input | What it does |
|---|---|
| **W A S D** | Move around |
| **Mouse** | Look around |
| **Left click** (or **E**) | Interact with whatever you're looking at — flip the lamp, open a drawer, rotate things, pick stuff up, pull the lever |
| **Esc** | Quit |

You don't need anything fancy — just walk up to an object, look at it, and click.
